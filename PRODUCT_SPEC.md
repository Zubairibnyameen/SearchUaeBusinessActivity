## Product Positioning

"Search UAE Business Activities. Find Jurisdictions. Understand Licences. Discover Approvals. Check Regulatory Costs."

NOT a generic company-formation agency website. A serious professional data/intelligence platform.

---

## FRONTEND HERO SEARCH — CRITICAL REQUIREMENT

The primary purpose of this platform is searching UAE business activities.

The homepage HERO SECTION MUST contain the main activity search bar.

The search bar must NOT be hidden inside another page.

A user should be able to land on the homepage and immediately search for their business activity without clicking through multiple pages.

### Hero Content

**Headline:**
"Find the Right UAE Jurisdiction for Your Business Activity"

**Subheadline:**
"Search UAE business activities and discover matching mainland and free-zone jurisdictions, licences, approvals and verified government fees."

**Search Prompt:**
"What business do you want to start?"

**Search Placeholder:**
"Search your business activity or describe your business idea..."

**Example Searches:**
- Digital Marketing Agency
- Amazon Seller
- Restaurant
- AI Software Company
- Accounting Consultancy
- Import Clothing from India
- Social Media Marketing Agency

The search must be the most visually prominent interactive element on the homepage.

### User Capabilities

1. Type a keyword
2. Type a natural-language business description
3. Submit with Enter
4. Click the search button
5. Receive activity results immediately

### Anti-Patterns (DO NOT)

- Do NOT make the homepage primarily a generic company-formation landing page
- The core product is the ACTIVITY SEARCH ENGINE
- Do NOT hide the search behind multiple pages
